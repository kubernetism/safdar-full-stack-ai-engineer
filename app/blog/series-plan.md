# Secure by Design: 10-part series plan

Based on the joint CISA, NSA, FBI and international partners' *Shifting the Balance of Cybersecurity Risk: Principles and Approaches for Secure by Design Software*. Page references refer to the supplied 36-page PDF. Each article will have an original cover image and a summary of 100 short points. Draft and review one article at a time.

1. **Secure by design: building security in** - Why security starts before development; the difference between design and defaults; shared responsibility. Pages 4-12. Drafted.
2. **Secure by default: the safer starting point** - Safe configurations, default security controls, tradeoffs, and customer experience. Pages 8-9, 30-31. Drafted.
3. **Who owns customer security?** - Manufacturer responsibility, hardening, security features, defaults, and outcomes. Pages 11-19. Drafted.
4. **Transparency and accountability** - Vulnerability reporting, accurate advisories, evidence, and customer trust. Pages 20-25.
5. **Security leadership starts at the top** - Executive sponsorship, organizational culture, incentives, and investment. Pages 26-27.
6. **Preventing vulnerabilities at their source** - Memory safety, secure components, root-cause fixes, and software development practices. Pages 28-29.
7. **Testing and disclosure throughout development** - Threat modeling, security review, testing, vulnerability reporting, and defense in depth. Pages 8, 20-25, 28-29.
8. **Secure defaults for identity and visibility** - Authentication, single sign-on, audit logs, and making core controls available. Pages 30-31.
9. **From hardening guides to loosening guides** - Secure baselines, optional changes, and clear explanations of risk. Page 32.
10. **Questions customers should ask vendors** - Procurement, supplier accountability, security evidence, and risk decisions. Pages 33-34.
